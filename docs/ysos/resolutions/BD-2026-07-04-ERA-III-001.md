## FROM SOFTWARE PROJECT TO AUTONOMOUS DIGITAL COMPANY

---

## Mission

The objective of YALIHAN OS is no longer software development.
The objective is **Business Automation**.

Every Sprint must increase the percentage of business operations completed autonomously.

**Success is measured by automation, not documentation.**

---

## Official Vision

> "Can YALIHAN automatically complete more real estate work today than it could yesterday?"

This becomes the **primary engineering objective**.

## Workspace Principle

Workspace is officially recognized as the **Digital Twin** of every managed property.

Everything belongs to Workspace:
- Portfolio
- CRM
- Drive
- Documents
- Publishing
- Finance
- Reservations
- AI

---

## AI Workforce

Hermes coordinates the complete Workforce.
Agents operate through Events.
**Never through direct coupling.**
Agent communication must remain Event Driven.

---

## Sprint Objective

Every Sprint must deliver **at least one measurable business capability**.

| Example | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Automatic Workspace Creation | System creates workspace automatically |
| Automatic Drive Creation | Documents synced to Drive |
| Automatic Property Scoring | Health score calculated |
| Automatic Publishing | Listings go live automatically |
| Automatic CRM Matching | Leads matched to properties |
| Automatic Notifications | Telegram + Email alerts |
